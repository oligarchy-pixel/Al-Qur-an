Do you know the angels who are at work around us every single moment? 🤍

Believing in the angels is the second pillar of faith. They were created from light, they never disobey Allah subhanahu wa ta'ala, and they always carry out what they are commanded.

✨ The 10 angels every Muslim must believe in:
1. Jibril — delivers revelation to the prophets and messengers
2. Mikail — oversees provision, rain, and thunder
3. Israfil — will blow the Trumpet on the Day of Judgement
4. Izrail — takes the souls of those whose time has come
5. Munkar — questions every person in the grave
6. Nakir — questions every person in the grave alongside Munkar
7. Raqib — records every good deed
8. Atid — records every bad deed
9. Malik — guards the gates of Hellfire
10. Ridwan — guards the gates of Paradise

🌙 And other groups of angels: Kiraman Katibin, Zabaniyah, Harut and Marut, Darda'il, Hamalatul 'Arsh, Hafazhah, Jundallah, Arham, Haffun, and Khazanah.

Every deed is written. Every step is guarded. May this awareness make us more mindful in everything we do.

"They all believe in Allah, His angels, His books, and His messengers." (Surah Al-Baqarah, verse 285)
