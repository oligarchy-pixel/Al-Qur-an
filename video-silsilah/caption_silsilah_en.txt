CAPTION
Do you know the noble lineage of our beloved Prophet Muhammad shalallahu alaihi wasallam? 🤍

The Messenger of Allah shalallahu alaihi wasallam said:
"Indeed, Allah chose Kinanah from the descendants of Ismail alaihissalam, chose Quraysh from Kinanah, chose Banu Hashim from Quraysh, and chose me from Banu Hashim." (Sahih Muslim, number 2276)

🌿 His closest family:
• Father: Abdullah bin Abdul Muttalib (passed away before the Prophet was born)
• Mother: Aminah bint Wahb (passed away when he was six years old)
• Grandfather: Abdul Muttalib, whose real name was Shaybah

✨ The lineage, generation by generation:
• From his father Abdullah to Adnan — agreed upon by all the scholars
• From Adnan to Prophet Ismail alaihissalam and Prophet Ibrahim alaihissalam
• From Prophet Ibrahim alaihissalam back to Prophet Nuh, Prophet Idris, Prophet Shith, and Prophet Adam alaihimussalam, the father of all mankind

Fifty generations, one chosen line — ending with the Seal of the Prophets shalallahu alaihi wasallam.

"Indeed, Allah and His angels send blessings upon the Prophet." (Surah Al-Ahzab, verse 56)
Allahumma shalli 'ala Muhammad wa 'ala ali Muhammad. 🤲
