TITLE
The First Night in the Grave — What Happens After They Walk Away

ALTERNATIVE TITLES
- Your First Night in Barzakh
- The Moment Everyone Leaves Your Grave
- 3 Questions Every Soul Will Be Asked


CAPTION
One day, they will lower us gently into the earth… and then walk away. 🤍

The Messenger of Allah shalallahu alaihi wasallam told us that the deceased hears the footsteps of those who leave after the burial. Then two angels come, and three questions are asked:

🕯️ Who is your Lord?
🕯️ What is your religion?
🕯️ Who is this man who was sent among you?

No notes. No time to revise. Only the faith we lived by will answer, by the permission of Allah.

"And behind them is a barrier until the Day they are resurrected." (Surah Al-Mu'minun, verse 100)

When the Prophet shalallahu alaihi wasallam finished burying someone, he would say:
"Seek forgiveness for your brother and ask for him to be made firm, for he is now being questioned." (Sunan Abi Dawud, number 3221)

Do not busy yourself guessing the state of those who have passed. Pray for them. And prepare for your own journey — your grave is being built today, by what you do.

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ عَذَابِ الْقَبْرِ
"O Allah, I seek refuge in You from the punishment of the grave."
